USE CASE 03

Customer Segmentation

Discovering groups of customers with similar behaviors and characteristics.

PROBLEM CONTEXT

The Problem

Companies may have thousands of customers with different purchasing behaviors, preferences, and spending patterns.

Treating every customer in exactly the same way may not be effective. Machine Learning can identify groups of customers with similar characteristics.

Main Question
Which customers have similar behaviors?
DATA

Data That Could Be Used

Purchase Frequency

Number of purchases made by a customer during a specific period.

Spending

Total amount spent by each customer.

Customer Activity

Visits, interactions, product preferences, and other behavioral information.

MODEL OBJECTIVE

Discover Groups

The model should identify groups of customers that share similar characteristics without predefined categories.

Discover hidden patterns in customer behavior.
MACHINE LEARNING TYPE

Unsupervised Learning

This problem can be addressed with Unsupervised Learning because predefined customer categories are not required.

Clustering
POTENTIAL BENEFITS

Why Use Machine Learning?

Personalization

Create more relevant experiences based on customer behavior.

Marketing

Design strategies for different customer groups.

Business Insights

Discover patterns that may not be obvious through manual analysis.

Key Takeaway

Unsupervised Learning can discover groups of customers with similar characteristics without requiring predefined categories.
