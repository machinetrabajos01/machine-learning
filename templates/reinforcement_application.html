STEP 03 · ENVIRONMENT LOGIC

Rules

  • Four actions: {{ actions|join(", ") }}.
  • The agent cannot leave the grid or cross a #; it stays in the same cell.
  • The agent can enter a D, but pays the penalty.
  • The episode ends at T or after {{ config.max_steps }} steps.
  • Every step returns: next state, cell type, reward and whether the episode ended.
